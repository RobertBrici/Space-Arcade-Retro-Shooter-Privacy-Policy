Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-15

Controller and contact

Space-Arcade-Retro-Shooter (published on Google Play under the developer display name Sticky Joystick Arcade) is operated by Brici Robert, who is the data controller responsible for the app's own processing of personal data. For privacy requests or questions, contact robert.brici@outlook.com. No EU representative is currently designated. If a company or other legal entity will publish the app in the future, this controller identity will be updated before release.

Data handled by the app

The app does not require an account and has no operator-run server. It stores the following information in Android DataStore on your device:

  • Game state, including lives, life-regeneration time, high score, and your five best run scores.
  • Purchase-entitlement flags returned by Google Play Billing. The app does not store payment-card details or purchase tokens.
  • The selected gameplay background, along with the locally cached background entitlements returned by Google Play.
  • Your optional rewarded-ad choice and accessibility preferences.
  • Rewarded-ad completion timestamps used to determine eligibility in a rolling 60-minute window, plus the temporary-reward expiry and the local date on which it was claimed.

The device clock and time zone are read on the device to calculate regeneration and time-limited reward eligibility. The app stores the relevant regeneration and ad-completion timestamps and the reward-claim date; it does not send the device's time-zone setting to an operator server. Expired ad-completion timestamps are removed when the app next performs its periodic local cleanup. If the app is not running, expired timestamps can remain on the device until it runs again or local data is deleted.

No analytics SDK is enabled and no analytics events are sent. The app does not request or store your date of birth or age, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location. Personal run records remain on your device; the app has no multiplayer or global leaderboard.

Purposes and legal bases

The app uses locally stored game and preference data to provide the gameplay and settings you request. Where this information is personal data, the operator relies on the steps needed to provide the app service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Optional advertising is only requested when the in-app advertising setting permits it and Google's consent information allows a request. Where consent is the applicable legal basis, it is sought through the in-app choice and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) flow (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and applicable ePrivacy rules). No analytics processing currently takes place.

Third-party services and disclosures

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): used for optional rewarded ads. Every ad request is configured for child-directed, age-restricted treatment and a G-rated maximum ad content rating. The app removes the Android advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox ad-services permissions. The SDK may still process IP address, approximate location inferred from network data, device/app information, diagnostics, and ad delivery or interaction data under Google's policies. Requests are gated by your in-app choice and UMP's permission to request ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): obtains and applies advertising consent information and presents consent or privacy options. UMP keeps its own consent state separately from the app's DataStore.
  • Google Play Billing: handles product purchases and purchase queries. The app receives product and ownership status to provide the associated game entitlement; Google Play handles payment credentials.

The app's operator does not receive payment details or advertising identifiers through its own server. Google services may process data under Google's own roles, policies, and retention practices, and may transfer it internationally. See Google's Privacy Policy, and Google's advertising information. The app operator does not run analytics or sell data from an operator-run server.

Choices, withdrawal, and children

You can decline advertising and continue playing. Turn off Allow rewarded advertising in Settings to stop the app from requesting future rewarded ads. Use Advertising privacy options in Settings to manage choices handled by UMP and Google. Changing an app setting does not erase information Google already processed. If you withdraw consent, that does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

The game is intended for players aged 7 and older. It does not collect age or date-of-birth information; instead, all ad requests receive the child-directed, age-restricted treatment and G-rated content limit. A parent or guardian should review the optional advertising choice and any Google consent form. The publisher declares the actual target audience and content rating in Google Play Console in accordance with applicable Families requirements.

Retention and deletion

Game state, scores, accessibility settings, and in-app consent choices remain in DataStore in app-private persistent storage, not the cache directory. Clearing only the app cache does not delete these records. They are deleted if you use Delete local data, clear app storage/data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. These items have no shorter automatic expiry. The app-managed DataStore and shared-preference files are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so they are not restored after app data is cleared or the app is reinstalled.

The in-app deletion action clears the app-managed DataStore, including local personal records, lives, ad-completion timestamps, cached entitlement flags, the selected gameplay background, and the app's advertising and accessibility choices. It does not reset UMP's separately stored consent state, cancel purchases, or delete information held by Google. Google Play purchase entitlements may be fetched again from Google Play when purchases are queried. Google Play purchase history must be managed through Google. Android's Clear cache action only removes cache files and does not erase the DataStore records described above. To delete app data and app-private SDK data, use Android's Clear storage or Clear data action, or uninstall the app; information already held by Google may still remain under Google's retention rules.
